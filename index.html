Our first campaign / private jet fuel tax

Let’s end the private jet fuel tax break.

You pay the ordinary fuel-excise rate to get to work. Domestic jet fuel is taxed at about one-fifteenth of that rate.

We think that should change. And we want people to act together to change it.

A message you can personalise

Subject: End the fuel-excise disparity for private aviation

Dear [Minister / MP’s name],

I’m asking you to support ending the lower domestic fuel-excise treatment for non-commercial private aviation. People buying petrol or diesel for ordinary commuting face a higher rate per litre than private jet aviation. I don’t think that is fair.

Will you support reform that ends this disparity, protects essential services such as aeromedical and firefighting flights, and maintains aviation safety funding?

Please explain your position and what action you will take. I would appreciate a written reply.

[Your name]
[Your suburb or town]

Where does the aviation excise go?

The Parliamentary Budget Office explains that aviation fuel-excise revenue is provided to CASA to fund aviation safety regulation. Ordinary fuel excise principally raises revenue for the federal budget.

Safety regulation benefits the public. Our argument is about the different tax rates, not a claim that CASA funding is money returned to jet owners.
