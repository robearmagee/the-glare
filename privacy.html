Your email and privacy

Your details stay yours.

We send occasional policy campaign emails, starting with climate. We won’t sell or rent your details, or share them for anyone else’s marketing. You can unsubscribe anytime.

Who is responsible

The Glare is an independent campaign and manages this mailing list. For privacy questions, corrections, requests to delete your details or concerns about how your information is handled, email theglare@robmagee.com.au.

What you’re joining

The Glare brings people together to push for smarter policy in Australia. We’re starting with practical climate action and the private jet fuel tax campaign. Emails may include campaign progress, practical actions and invitations to contribute ideas to this and future policy campaigns. Joining the email list does not sign a petition or authorise us to contact a minister in your name.

Information we use

The form asks for your email address and permission to send campaign emails. Brevo also processes subscription and confirmation records, delivery information and technical details needed to operate and protect the form, such as IP addresses. If you reply, we receive the information you choose to include.

Confirmation emails check that the address belongs to you. We use subscription information to send the emails you requested, manage delivery and honour unsubscribes. We don’t require your phone number, address or political affiliation.

The services involved

Brevo manages the form, subscriber list and email delivery. It reports that its databases are hosted in the European Union, including France, Germany and Belgium. Email delivery and supporting service providers can involve processing outside Australia. See Brevo’s privacy policy and data storage information.

Cloudflare Turnstile checks for automated spam. It processes technical browser and connection information for that check. See Cloudflare’s Turnstile privacy information.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and processes connection information when serving it. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Subscriber lists are not published in the website’s public source files.

Measurement

This website has no advertising pixels or additional audience analytics. The signup and spam prevention services process technical information as described above. Brevo email features can record deliveries, opens and link clicks; email open measurements are not always accurate. We do not use this information for other people’s marketing.

Unsubscribe and control your details

Use the unsubscribe link in a campaign email to stop campaign messages. You can also contact the organiser to ask about your information, request a correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern.

We retain subscription information while it is needed to run the list. After you unsubscribe, a minimal record may be retained to ensure you are not added back accidentally, along with consent or operational records where necessary. Deletion requests also need to account for service provider backup and retention processes.

Other websites and changes

Links to ministers, evidence and other websites lead to services with their own privacy practices. If we change how we handle subscriber information, we will update this notice and seek fresh permission where the new use requires it.
